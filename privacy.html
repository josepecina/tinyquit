Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. Versión en español

1. Who is responsible

Jose Peciña Usina (Tiny Apps), with tax ID 51506314E and address at calle Ramón Power 42, 28043 Madrid (Spain), is the controller of your data in TinyQuit. Contact: hellotinyapps@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

Your plan data: how many cigarettes, sticks or puffs you log, at what time, your limit, your pace, your difficult moments, the reasons you note down, the price and your preferences. This data is considered health-related. If you create an account (it's optional), also your email address and the identifier Google (and, in the future, Apple) gives you when you sign in. In addition, minimal technical device data so the app can work.

3. Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your phone and we don't receive it, unless you turn on sending anonymous data (section 4 bis). If your phone backup is turned on (Google or iCloud), the system itself may store an encrypted copy of the app data in your Google or Apple account; that copy is managed by them, not by us.

If you create an account (optional, with Google): we keep a copy of your progress (your plan, your history, your worlds and your settings) linked to your account, on Supabase servers located in the European Union, so you can recover it or use it on another phone. Only you can access it with your session. If you sign out, the copy stays on the server until you delete the account.

4. Why and on what legal basis

To provide the service (performance of a contract) and, for health data, with your explicit consent, which you give when you start using the app and, if you create an account, again before saving your progress on the server. To send you notifications on your phone, with the permission you grant on your device. To send you emails with tips and news, only if you tick the corresponding box. We never use your consumption data for advertising and we never sell it.

4 bis. Anonymous data to improve the app (optional)

Only if you turn it on (box at the start or «Help us improve» in Settings), the app sends usage statistics to understand what helps people smoke less: a random identifier created on your phone, the app version, the language, what you track (tobacco, IQOS or vape), each day's summary (how many, your limit, whether it was green), the reasons you note down and the feedback you send us from «Send us feedback». We don't send your name, email, location or the exact time of each cigarette, and the identifier is not linked to your account. Legal basis: your explicit consent; you can withdraw it at any time from Settings and we'll stop receiving data. If it's turned off, we'll remind you at most once a year. This data is stored on Supabase (servers in the European Union) for a maximum of 24 months. As the identifier is random, we can't locate your previous submissions unless you give it to us from Settings.

5. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. To show and measure them, Google may use your phone's advertising ID and technical device data. If they are personalised, we'll ask your permission first. Ads are never based on your consumption data and will never be for tobacco, vaping or related products. With Premium there are no automatic ads: you'll only see one if you choose to, for example to get a shield or unlock an achievement.

6. How long we keep it

As long as you have the app or the account. If you delete your account from Settings, your account and your saved progress are removed from our servers immediately; if you ask us by email (see delete your account), within 30 days at most, except for what the law requires us to keep. You manage the system backup from your phone's settings or your Google or Apple account. The anonymous data in section 4 bis, as that section states.

7. Who we share it with

Only with providers that help us provide the service (hosting, anonymous statistics database, email sending, sign-in with Apple or Google), under contracts that require them to protect your data. If any of them is outside the European Economic Area, appropriate safeguards apply, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Specifically: Google (Google Play, Premium payments and AdMob ads), RevenueCat (manages your Premium subscription with a random identifier, without consumption data) and Supabase (your progress, if you create an account, and anonymous statistics, only if you turn them on; hosted in the EU).

8. Your rights

You can access your data, rectify it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, request its portability and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to hellotinyapps@gmail.com. From Settings you can export and delete your data. If you think we haven't respected your rights, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

9. Minimum age

TinyQuit is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10. Security and changes

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data. If we change this policy, we'll let you know in the app.
