TinyQuit

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Jose Peciña Usina (Tiny Apps), tax ID 51506314E, calle Ramón Power 42, 28043 Madrid (Spain), is the data controller for TinyQuit. Contact: hellotinyapps@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

The data of your plan: how many cigarettes, sticks or puffs you log, at what time, your limit, your pace, your difficult moments, the reasons you write down, the price and your preferences. This data is considered health-related. If you create an account (optional), also your email address and the identifier Google or Apple gives you when you sign in. Plus minimal technical device data so the app works.

3. Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your phone and we do not receive it, except in the optional cases explained below (account, anonymous data, feedback and codes). If you save a backup from Settings («Save backup»), a file is created that you keep wherever you choose; it does not reach us. If your phone’s backup is on, the system itself (Google on Android, Apple iCloud on iPhone) may keep an encrypted copy of the app’s data in your account; that copy is managed by Google or Apple, not by us. If you use the watch app, your phone and watch exchange data directly, without going through our servers.

If you create an account (optional, with Google or Apple): we keep a copy of your progress (your plan, history, worlds and settings) linked to your account, on Supabase servers located in the European Union, so you can recover it or use it on another phone. Only you can access it with your session. If you sign out, the copy stays on the server until you delete the account.

4. Purposes and legal basis

While your data is only on your phone, we do not process it. When it reaches us, it is always because of something you choose and with your explicit consent: when creating an account (we ask before saving your progress on the server), when turning on the anonymous data in section 4a, or when sending us feedback. To send you notifications on your phone we use the permission you grant on your device. We do not send you emails. We never use your consumption data for advertising and we never sell it.

4a. Anonymous data to improve the app (optional)

Only if you turn it on (checkbox at the start or «Help us improve» in Settings), the app sends usage statistics to understand what helps people smoke less: a random identifier created on your phone, the app version, the language, what you count (tobacco, IQOS or vape), each day’s summary (how many, your limit, whether it was green) and the reasons you write down. We do not send your name, email, location or the exact time of each cigarette, and the identifier is not linked to your account. Legal basis: your explicit consent; you can withdraw it at any time in Settings and we will stop receiving data. If it is off, we will remind you at most once a year. This data is stored on Supabase (EU servers) for up to 24 months. Because the identifier is random, we cannot find your previous submissions unless you give it to us from Settings.

4b. Feedback, surveys and codes (optional)

If you send us feedback from «Give us your opinion» or answer the testers’ survey, we keep what you write and choose, together with your phone’s random identifier, the app version and the language, to read it and improve the app. If you redeem a code (for example, a tester or gift code), we keep the code and that random identifier so each code is used the allowed number of times. The app also checks a general setting of ours from time to time (for example, whether the tester period has ended), without sending your data. All of this is stored on Supabase (EU) for up to 24 months.

5. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. To show and measure them, Google may use your phone’s advertising identifier and technical device data. On iPhone, only if you accept Apple’s «Allow the app to track» prompt; otherwise ads are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we first show Google’s consent notice, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → «Ad privacy». Ads are never based on your consumption data and will never be for tobacco, vaping or related products. With Premium there are no automatic ads: you only see one if you choose it, for example to get a shield or unlock an achievement.

6. How long we keep it

As long as you keep the app or the account. If you delete your account from Settings, your account and saved progress are removed from our servers immediately; if you ask us by email (see delete your account), within 30 days at most, except what the law requires us to keep. You delete the backup file yourself. Data from sections 4a and 4b, as stated there.

7. Who we share it with

Only with providers that help us deliver the service, under contracts that oblige them to protect your data. If any of them is outside the European Economic Area, appropriate safeguards apply, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Specifically: Google (Google Play, Premium payments on Android, AdMob ads and Sign in with Google), Apple (App Store, Premium payments on iPhone and Sign in with Apple), RevenueCat (manages your Premium subscription with a random identifier, or your account’s if you are signed in, with no consumption data) and Supabase (optional account, anonymous data, feedback, surveys and codes, hosted in the European Union).

8. Your rights

You can access, rectify or erase your data, object to or restrict its use, request portability and withdraw your consent at any time by writing to hellotinyapps@gmail.com. From Settings you can export and delete your data. If you believe we have not respected your rights, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

9. Minimum age

TinyQuit is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10. Security and changes

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data. If we change this policy, we will let you know in the app.

11. If you use TinyQuit on iPhone

On iPhone you can sign in with your Apple account ("Sign in with Apple") or with Google. With Apple we only receive an identifier and the email you choose to share, which can be a hidden Apple address. Premium purchases are handled by Apple in the App Store: we never see your payment details. Before showing personalised ads we ask for your permission with Apple's tracking prompt (ATT); if you don't give it, ads are not personalised. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
